Display

Orientation, theme and the Glass look.

Screen

Calm: a ripple on touch, a glow when a tile turns on, pages crossfade and cards grow out of their tile. Water: drop rings on touch, the on colour floods out of your finger, a water edge between pages and cards appear in a spreading circle. Glass: a highlight under your finger and a springy release, a glossy sheen when a tile turns on, pages slide over each other in depth and cards rise like a glass sheet. Heavy (costs more): the tile bends like water under your finger, the fill drifts through aurora colours for a moment, tiles change page column by column and cards grow out of their tile. Custom: pick each one yourself.

Pictures

Add pictures separately for each orientation. This browser crops them to fit and stores JPEGs on the device. Glass uses the old single picture or a built-in gradient when a gallery is empty. Show now pins a picture for the next boot. Config backups include the settings, not the pictures.

Daily downloads Windows Spotlight pictures once a day. Internal storage replaces the previous set; microSD keeps an archive. Both mixes daily and your own pictures.

Counts above 16 require a mounted microSD card. For each orientation, 12 take about 5 minutes. Swipes can stutter meanwhile; a thin line at the top of the screen shows the download.

Brightness

day

--

Brightness when Home Assistant turns on the tactula Night switch, or when the optional local schedule is active. Set to 0 to turn the backlight off; a touch briefly lights it at 10%.

Matching start and end times disable the night interval.

TimeThe schedule uses the timezone set on the Time page. Until network time is available, day brightness remains active.

Sleep

-

Without touch and without presence. 0 = never.

Shows the wallpaper gallery (Wallpapers) in any theme. At night it uses the night brightness; the screen turns off when that is 0 or the gallery is empty. Pictures on a microSD card: enable it in Maintenance > microSD card.

--

Motion, occupancy or a person: any of them "on" or "home" keeps the screen awake, and its arrival wakes it, also after Off in the quick panel. A touch always wakes it; that first touch does nothing else.

Maintenance

Back up device settings, install firmware, set up the microSD card, or return the monitor to first-run setup.

Configuration backup

Export the deck pages and tiles plus the display, LED, time, network and MQTT settings. WiFi network names, passwords and the Home Assistant token are never included. Import preserves WiFi and Home Assistant credentials. The MQTT password is kept only when its broker host and username are unchanged.

InfoImport accepts tactula-compatible backup files, validates every setting, and restarts the device after a successful restore.

Internal flash storage

Stores your pages and tiles (deck.json), icon font, wallpapers and other files in the ESP32's own flash memory. No microSD card is needed to save pages.

Checking internal flash...

Warning The internal filesystem cannot mount. This may be new, unformatted flash, or it may contain files from an older installation. Initialization permanently erases its contents, including any existing pages, icons and pictures. It does not erase Home Assistant or Wi-Fi credentials in NVS. Do not initialize if you need to recover old files.

microSD card

Holds the wallpaper gallery (also shown by the photo frame), the daily picture archive and brand icons. Without a card, pictures go to internal storage and brand icons are not available. The card uses GPIO 10-13. Pictures already stored stay where they are.

Format card erases the card and creates one FAT partition the deck can read. Use it when the card answers but its file system is not accepted.

Custom PNG icons

Upload transparent PNG icons straight to the microSD card, without removing it. Use lowercase filenames such as netflix_on.png and netflix_off.png. Icons are shared across tactula variants; existing files remain in /touchdeck/icons/bedroom/ for compatibility.

Checking microSD card...

Maximum 512 KB and 512×512 pixels per icon. Existing names are replaced. PNG display on the touchscreen will be added separately; this section currently manages the files.
